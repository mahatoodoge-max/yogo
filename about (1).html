About Riverbend Yoga Studio

Meet Our Lead Instructor

Maya Chen founded Riverbend Yoga Studio after a decade of teaching in community centers and physical therapy clinics. She is certified in trauma-informed and chair-adapted yoga, and she leads our Gentle Flow and Restorative classes.

Accessibility Accommodations

Our studio floor is step-free, and chairs, blocks, straps, and bolsters are available for every class at no extra cost. Let us know in advance about any mobility, vision, or hearing needs so we can prepare a spot that works for you.

Studio Policies

  • Please arrive 10-15 minutes before class to check in and set up.
  • Cancel or reschedule private sessions at least 24 hours in advance.
  • Mats are provided, but you are welcome to bring your own.
  • Scent-free products are appreciated, as some students have sensitivities.
