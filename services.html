    Private Sessions

    One-on-one sessions are available for students who want personalized attention, are working around an injury, or prefer a quieter setting. Sessions can be booked in-studio or, weather permitting, outdoors along the river path.

    Pricing Overview

    Drop-in classes generally range from $18 to $24. Monthly unlimited memberships range from $95 to $130. Private sessions are priced separately. Contact us for current rates; online checkout is not available.
