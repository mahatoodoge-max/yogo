Welcome to Riverbend Yoga Studio

Our Mission

Riverbend Yoga Studio offers a calm, inclusive space where people of every age and ability can build strength, ease stress, and move at their own pace. We believe yoga should feel welcoming, not intimidating.

New to Yoga? You're Welcome Here

Never rolled out a mat before? No problem. Our instructors offer modifications for every pose, and beginners are welcome in any class marked "all levels." Arrive fifteen minutes early and we will help you get settled.

Featured Class
